NASA Historical Photograph Slots — BEYOND RELICS
================================================

Place real historical NASA photographs or archive images in the corresponding item subfolder:
- /public/explore/nasa/apollo-guidance-computer/
- /public/explore/nasa/kepler-space-telescope/
- /public/explore/nasa/sojourner-rover/
- /public/explore/nasa/lunar-roving-vehicle/
- /public/explore/nasa/pioneer-10-11/

To activate an uploaded photograph, set the matching `src` field in `src/data/nasaTechnology.ts`
(for example: `src: '/explore/nasa/lunar-roving-vehicle/apollo15-lrv.jpg'`).
Leave `src` empty until the real photograph is added so the labeled archive placeholder appears cleanly.
